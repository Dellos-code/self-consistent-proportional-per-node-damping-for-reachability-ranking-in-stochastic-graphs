\chapter{Mixing strategies for the dual-operator hybrid}\n\label{ch:mixing}\n\nThis chapter reports the preregistered mixing study. Selection was performed on the dev panel only, with independent evaluation ground truth used for scoring. The ranked configurations and exact configuration identifiers are stored in the accompanying CSV and JSONL files.\n\nThe s3_dev screen evaluated 2835000 configuration-instance rows. The best observed mean Spearman correlation was 0.9133.\n
